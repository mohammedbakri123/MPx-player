# Code of Conduct

MPx Player is a small community project. Be kind, be patient, be honest.

## We expect

- Respect for other people, whatever their experience level, language, or background.
- Feedback about the *work* ("this scrolls badly on my phone, here's a recording"), not about the person.
- Credit where it's due, and no passing off other people's work as your own.

## We don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic links — especially download links to other APKs, "cracked" apps, or anything pirated.
- Sharing someone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this, report it in a private message to the maintainer via GitHub, or open an issue and ask the maintainer to contact you. Reports are handled privately.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may warn, hide, or remove offending content, and may block repeat offenders from the repo, Discussions, and the Telegram group. Decisions are final and not debated in public threads.

*Adapted from the Contributor Covenant — kept short on purpose for a one-maintainer project.*
