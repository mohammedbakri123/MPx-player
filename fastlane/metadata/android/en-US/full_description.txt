MPx Player is a free, open-source media player for Android, built
in native Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and powered by mpv for
playback.

No ads. No tracking. Your files stay on your device.

Features:
- Local video and music, folders, playlists, history and favourites
- Wide codec support via mpv, including files other players refuse
- External subtitles (.srt, .ass, .ssa, .vtt) that stay selected
- Media Information: codec, resolution, FPS, bitrate, audio and
  subtitle tracks, from the player or by long-pressing a video
- Picture-in-Picture, double-tap to skip, resume where you left off
- Play a video straight from a pasted URL
- True-black dark theme + light theme, translated into 9 languages
